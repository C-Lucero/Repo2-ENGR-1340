When adding fractions, the most important thing is to look at the denominators (bottom numbers):
ex: (1/4) + (2/4) 
Since the denominators are already the same, we can simply add the numerators (top numbers):
so (1/4) + (2/4) = (3/4)
What if the denominators are different? Find a common denominator
Ex: (1/2) + (1/3)
The smallest number that both 2 and 3 divide into is 6.
Convert both fractions-
(1/2) = (3/6)
(1/3) = (2/6)
Now add (3/6) + (2/6) = (5/6)

Therefore: (1/2) + (1/3) = (5/6)
